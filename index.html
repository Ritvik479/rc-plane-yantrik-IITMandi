// Aerodynamic Design

Design Calculations

✦
Airfoil — CH-10
Selected for high CL and predictable stall behavior in cargo-lift applications. Provides superior lift at the low airspeeds required for stable payload delivery.
⊕
CG Position
Positioned at 25–30% of wing MAC (70–84 mm from the leading edge), ensuring a positive static margin of ~10% for predictable longitudinal stability.
↑
Thrust-to-Weight
1.1 with payload, ensuring adequate climb performance during the mission. Drops to 0.9 without payload — still sufficient for level cruise and landing.
◈
Wing Loading
~4.19 kg/m² (~41 N/m²), enabling a very low stall speed and gentle flight characteristics — critical for accurate, controlled payload drops.
⟁
High-Wing + Dihedral
The high-wing monoplane configuration combined with a 3° dihedral provides inherent roll stability and self-righting tendencies for safe cargo missions.
⊞
Simulation Tools
Geometry modelled in OpenVSP. Airfoil and wing aerodynamics validated in XFLR5. Full 3D CAD designed in SolidWorks.

// Payload Delivery System

Mission Payload

  • 01
    250g Cube Payload
    Housed in a balsa cage structure internal to the fuselage, centered exactly at the aircraft CG to ensure no trim change during drop.
  • 02
    Trap Door Mechanism
    RC-operated trap door driven by a high-torque 9g servo. Opens on command to release the payload at the target drop zone.
  • 03
    Push Spring Ejection
    Springs mounted at the roof of the cage assist ejection, ensuring clean payload separation and consistent drop behaviour.
  • 04
    CG-Neutral Design
    Payload bay placement at aircraft CG means balance is maintained before, during, and after the drop — no re-trimming required.

// Mission Planning

Competition Strategy

Qualifier Round
Focus on maximum flight time and sustained stability with the 250g payload onboard. Demonstrates airframe reliability and control authority under load.
Main Round
Prioritize precision payload drop by timing the servo release based on real-time groundspeed estimates and approach angle to the designated drop zone.
Safety Protocol
Pre-flight checklist includes range testing, servo travel verification, and fail-safe return-to-home validation before every flight attempt.
